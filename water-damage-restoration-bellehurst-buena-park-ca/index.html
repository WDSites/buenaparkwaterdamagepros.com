Bellehurst is the name the City of Buena Park uses for an area on the north side of town, near the Los Coyotes Country Club and Ralph B. Clark Regional Park. If water is coming through a ceiling, spreading from a failed supply line or backing up from a drain in this part of Buena Park, this page explains how restoration works and what to do first.

We cover Bellehurst as part of our Buena Park service area. The practical details below come from city documents and public records, and we say plainly where a fact could not be confirmed.

Where Bellehurst is and how we describe it

The city General Plan environmental report refers to the "Bellehurst area" near the Los Coyotes Country Club, and separately to the "Bellehurst neighborhood" near the city's northwest boundary. The Los Coyotes Monument stands at 8888 Los Coyotes Drive, which sits in ZIP 90621, and the same report notes that it marks the site of an 1847 military encampment.

We have not found official boundary lines for Bellehurst, so we do not draw them here. If you are unsure whether your street counts as Bellehurst, that does not matter for service. Give the dispatcher your street address and they will confirm coverage.

What kind of homes are in this part of Buena Park

Listing sites describe a mix of detached houses and attached townhomes and condominiums around the country club, with homeowner association fees on many of the attached units. Those descriptions come from commercial listing data that nobody has verified for us, so treat them as a general picture and not as a survey.

City figures give a wider view. Buena Park had about 25,000 housing units in 2020, and roughly two thirds were single family homes. The city housing element says most of the housing stock was built in the 1950s and 1960s. That is a citywide statistic, not a Bellehurst figure.

Townhomes and HOA buildings: who does what when a pipe fails

In an attached building, water rarely stays in one unit. A leaking supply line in an upstairs bathroom can reach the ceiling below, and the wall between two homes can carry moisture sideways. If you live in a townhome or condominium, call your HOA manager or property manager as soon as you know the source.

Ask who controls the shutoff for your unit and whether the association has its own insurance for the building shell. Your own policy usually covers your interior, so ask both sides in writing what they will treat. Dried walls and floors in a shared building need coordination, because one unit cannot dry properly if its neighbor stays wet.

Single family homes: the common ways water gets in

In a detached home the usual causes are a split supply line, a failed water heater, a dishwasher or washing machine hose, a leaking roof after a winter storm, or a sewer line that backs up into a floor drain or shower. Each one calls for a different response, and sewage needs far more care than clean supply water.

We cannot tell you how many Bellehurst homes have slab foundations or raised foundations. Both exist in Southern California, so check your own home. If you have a crawl space and water is under the house, say so on the call. It changes the equipment the crew needs.

First steps when water is spreading in Bellehurst

Stop the source if you safely can

Turn off the main water shutoff on your property if the leak is on a supply line. If it is an appliance, close the valve behind that appliance. Never wade through standing water to reach a valve if cords, outlets or the panel are wet.

Keep clear of electrical hazards

Do not touch outlets, switches or appliances in or near standing water. Do not flip breakers while standing in water. If you cannot reach the panel dry, leave it and tell the dispatcher so a professional can assess the electrical risk.

Protect people, pets and belongings

Keep children and pets away from any water that may have come from a toilet, drain or sewer line. Lift small valuables onto dry surfaces only where that is safe, and leave heavy furniture alone until the area is assessed.

Photograph, then call

Take wide and close photos of the damage and of the source, if you can do so safely. Then call (714) 515-1308 and give the dispatcher your address, what you can see and whether anyone is in a wet room.

How restoration works in a home like yours

A crew starts by finding the source and checking for safety hazards. Standing water is removed with extraction equipment. Moisture meters then show how far the water has traveled into drywall, framing, flooring and subfloor, and the crew sets air movers and dehumidifiers to dry the structure.

Drying is checked with repeat readings, not by touch alone. Materials that cannot be dried safely, such as saturated carpet padding or drywall that has stayed wet, may need to be removed. We do not promise that every material can be saved, and we explain what is salvageable after the readings. The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard is the industry reference for water damage work, so ask any company which standard its crews follow.

Insurance and documentation

Your insurer decides what is covered, based on the cause of the damage and the wording of your policy. Photos, moisture readings and an itemized description of the work can support a claim record, though none of it guarantees payment. Flood usually needs separate coverage. Protect the property reasonably after a loss, because a policy may not pay for damage that follows from delay.

Bellehurst: utilities, flood data and permits

Buena Park operates its own water utility. If the leak is upstream of your meter, report it to the city, and if it is downstream, it is yours to stop. OC San owns the regional sewer lines, and the lateral serving your home is usually the owner's responsibility.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified for Bellehurst. For the whole city, most land is mapped in the 500-year zone and inside the Prado Dam inundation area. Look up your own address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and ask the Building Division if a repair needs a permit.

Bellehurst questions

Do you serve Bellehurst in Buena Park?

We take calls from the north side of the city, including the homes and townhomes near the country club. Share your street address on the call so the dispatcher can confirm coverage.

What is the first thing to do about a leak in a Bellehurst townhome?

Stop the water if you can do so safely, then call your HOA manager or property manager so the building shutoff can be used. Photograph the damage and call us about drying. Neighboring units may be affected too.

Is the whole of Bellehurst in a flood zone?

We could not confirm FEMA status for Bellehurst as a whole. The maps are read lot by lot, so search your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for a firm answer.

Does water damage in an HOA building mean the association pays?

Not automatically. Who pays depends on the source of the water, the association documents and both insurance policies. Ask the association and your insurer in writing, and keep your photos and records.

Can I dry a wet floor myself with household fans?

Fans help with a small, clean spill, but they do not dry walls, subfloors or padding, and they are not enough for sewage or contaminated water. If water has gone beyond a surface spill, call for an assessment.

Should I worry about mold after a leak?

Wet drywall and padding can grow mold if they stay wet, which is why drying quickly matters. We cannot promise mold will never develop, and California does not license mold remediators, so ask any company what it does and how.
