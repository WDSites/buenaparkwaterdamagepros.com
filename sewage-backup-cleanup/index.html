Sewage in your home is Category 3 water, the most contaminated class there is. It can come up through a toilet, a tub drain or a floor drain, and it carries waste that does not belong near carpet, drywall or anyone's hands. Treat the room as off limits from the moment you see it and call our 24/7 line.

Backups happen when a line is blocked, cracked or overloaded, either on your property or out in the street. The cleanup has two parts that need two trades. A plumber or the sewer agency clears the blockage, and a restoration crew removes what the sewage touched and disinfects what is left.

One Fixture or Several? Read the Clue

Where the water appears tells you where the blockage likely is, and who to call first.

Either way, the contaminated cleanup is a separate job from the pipe repair.

Sewage Safety: What to Do Right Now

Keep people and pets out

Close the door to the room. Children, pets and anyone with a weak immune system should stay clear, and anyone with health questions should ask a physician.

Stop adding water

Do not flush, run the washer, or use sinks and showers. Every gallon you send down has nowhere to go but back up.

Leave fans and vacuums alone

A household vacuum or box fan spreads contaminated droplets and moisture through the house. Skip both.

Never mix cleaners

Do not combine bleach with ammonia or other cleaners. The fumes are dangerous. Gloves and boots are the minimum if you must enter, and a pro crew wears more.

Turn off power only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without stepping in the water. If the outlets near the sewage are wet, tell us on the call rather than testing them yourself.

How a Sewage Cleanup Is Run

The goal is to keep contamination where it is, take out what cannot be cleaned and treat the rest. Crews wear gloves, boots, eye protection, coveralls and respirators.

1

Contain the area

Plastic sheeting and containment barriers seal the room, and negative air machines pull air out so it does not drift into clean rooms.

2

Extract and remove

Extraction units pull liquid out. Porous materials such as carpet, pad, wet drywall and insulation are cut out, bagged and disposed of properly.

3

Clean and disinfect

Hard surfaces and framing are cleaned, then treated with a hospital-grade antimicrobial applied according to its label.

4

Dry and clear

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the structure, HEPA air scrubbers clean the air, and moisture meters confirm the readings before rebuilding starts.

Porous materials soak up sewage and cannot be made safe by surface cleaning, which is why carpet and pad usually go. Ask any company whether its crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500, the published industry reference for water damage, which sets out how Category 3 water is handled.

Insurance for Sewer Backups

Sewer and drain backup is often handled as a separate endorsement on a homeowners policy rather than included by default. Many standard policies do not cover it unless you added the coverage. Check your policy for words such as water backup, sewer backup or sump overflow, and ask your insurer before work begins.

The California Department of Insurance advises that insurers must follow the Fair Claims Settlement Practices Regulations deadlines to acknowledge and evaluate claims, and its consumer hotline is 1-800-927-4357. Photograph the damage first and keep every receipt.

Mistakes to Avoid

  • Do not keep flushing to see if it clears. It makes the backup larger.
  • Do not scrub carpet or leave wet padding in place. Sewage soaks into it.
  • Do not use a household wet vacuum on sewage. It sprays contamination into the air.
  • Do not wait on the plumber before calling a cleanup crew. Both jobs can start the same day.
  • Do not skip photos. Your insurer and landlord will ask for them.
  • Do not re-enter a treated room until the crew says it is cleared.

Common questions

Is emergency sewage backup cleanup available in Buena Park?

Yes. Our line is staffed 24/7. Call (714) 515-1308, tell us which fixtures are backing up and how far the water has spread, and we will give you safe first steps and schedule the cleanup. A plumber should clear the blockage at the same time.

Who clears the blockage, you or a plumber?

A plumber or the sewer agency clears the blocked line. We handle the cleanup: containment, removal of contaminated materials, disinfection and drying. The two jobs can run on the same day, and fixing the line first keeps the backup from returning.

Should I call the sewer agency or a plumber?

Start with a plumber. If only one fixture is affected, the clog is likely on your property. If several fixtures back up at once and the plumber finds the problem in the street line, notify the sewer agency. In this area, regional sewers are run by OC San.

Can I clean up a small sewage spill myself?

Even a small spill is Category 3 water, and porous materials such as carpet and pad cannot be made safe by wiping. If you must enter, wear gloves and boots, keep others out, and never mix bleach with other cleaners. For health questions, ask a physician.

Does insurance cover a sewer backup?

Often only if you added a water or sewer backup endorsement to your policy. Standard policies may exclude it, and flood is usually excluded as well. Check your policy and ask your insurer before work starts.

I rent. What should I do about sewage in my unit?

Photograph the damage, keep everyone out of the area, and tell your landlord in writing right away. Roughly 45 percent of Buena Park households rent, per Census data, so this comes up often. Your landlord or property manager decides who authorizes the cleanup.
